# Awesome Bar Chart Race [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of tools, libraries, datasets and tips for making **bar chart race** animations, the format behind
> millions of views on YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Reels.

## No-code tools
- [Zeyn Studio](https://studio.zeynelerek.com) – Free viral Shorts generator: vertical 1080×1920 bar chart races with music, 100+ ready templates, AI data from a prompt and one-tap YouTube upload. Renders in the browser.
- [Flourish](https://flourish.studio) – Popular web tool with a bar chart race template, great for embeds and landscape videos.

## Libraries
- [bar_chart_race](https://github.com/dexplo/bar_chart_race) – Python / matplotlib, renders MP4 or GIF from a pandas DataFrame.
- [pandas_alive](https://github.com/JackMcKew/pandas_alive) – Animated bar, line and pie charts from pandas.
- [raceplotly](https://github.com/lucharo/raceplotly) – Interactive bar chart races with Plotly.
- [gganimate](https://github.com/thomasp85/gganimate) – Animation grammar for ggplot2 (R).
- [D3 bar chart race](https://observablehq.com/@d3/bar-chart-race) – The classic Observable notebook by Mike Bostock.
- [Remotion](https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion) – Make videos programmatically with React; good for fully custom races.

## AI and automation
- [Zeyn Studio MCP](https://github.com/zeynmedia/zeyn-studio-mcp) – Let Claude Code, ChatGPT or Cursor create bar chart race videos through the Model Context Protocol.
- [Shorts factory](https://github.com/zeynmedia/zeynmedia-shorts-factory) – Open-source GitHub Actions pipeline that renders and uploads bar chart race Shorts on a schedule.

## Datasets
- [Sports bar chart race datasets](https://github.com/zeynmedia/sports-bar-chart-race-datasets) – Verified football, NBA, F1 and tennis stats as CSV/JSON.
- [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org) – Country-level time series (population, GDP, energy…).
- [World Bank Open Data](https://data.worldbank.org) – Economic indicators by country and year.

## Making them go viral
- Keep it short: 15–20 seconds gets the highest completion rate on Shorts.
- Pick a rivalry people argue about (Messi vs Ronaldo, Real vs Barça, Jordan vs LeBron).
- Put the question in the title with an emoji and let the final frame answer it.
- Use vivid gradients and big faces or logos; faded colors lose the scroll.
- More in [viral-shorts-ideas](https://github.com/zeynmedia/viral-shorts-ideas).
